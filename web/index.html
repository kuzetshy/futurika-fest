Questions about the festival

When and where is Futurika Fest 2027?

From Friday 21 to Sunday 23 May 2027 at the DOX Centre for Contemporary Art and DOX+ Hall, Poupětova 1, Prague 7. See directions.

Who is the festival for?

Software engineers and data scientists, digital creatives and media artists, and anyone curious about generative AI, including students.

How do I get a ticket?

Choose a 3-Day Pass, a Day Pass or a Student 3-Day Pass. Prices are listed under Tickets.
