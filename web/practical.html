♿ Accessibility & Inclusivity

  • Step-free access to all entrances and the DOX+ Hall.
  • Elevator access to Innovation Hub Floor 2.
  • Accessible restrooms on the Ground Floor and Floor 2.
  • Induction hearing loop installed in the Vision Hall.

🤝 Code of Conduct

Treat everyone with respect. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated. If you feel unsafe, tell a member of staff at the check-in desk.
