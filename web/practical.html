Practical Information & FAQ

Everything you need to know about tickets, venue access, transportation, and festival guidelines.

Location & Directions

DOX Centre for Contemporary Art & DOX+ Hall

  • Metro: Line C to Nádraží Holešovice (about a 5-minute walk)
  • Tram: Lines 6 and 12 to Ortenovo náměstí (about a 2-minute walk)

Accessibility & Inclusivity

  • Step-free access to all entrances and the DOX+ Hall.
  • Elevator access to Innovation Hub Floor 2.
  • Accessible restrooms on the Ground Floor and Floor 2.
  • Induction hearing loop installed in the Vision Hall.

Code of Conduct

Treat everyone with respect. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated. If you feel unsafe, tell a member of staff at the check-in desk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Accessibility & Facilities: can I reach both stages without steps?

Yes. The DOX+ Hall has step-free access, and an elevator connects the main lobby to the Tech & Code Lab on Floor 2.

Do I need to print my festival pass?

No printing is required. Show the QR code on your mobile device at the check-in desk at Poupětova 1.

How does My Schedule work?

Select “Save to Schedule” on any session. Your choices stay in this browser on this device, with no account needed.

Are laptop power outlets provided during workshops?

Yes. All seating rows in the Tech & Code Lab on Floor 2 have power strips with 230V European sockets.

Will sessions be recorded?

Keynotes in the Prompt & Vision Hall will be recorded and shared with ticket holders within 48 hours. Workshops are in person only.
