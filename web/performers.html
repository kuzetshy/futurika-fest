Questions about the performers

What do the Vision and Core AI labels mean?

They show the performer's track: Vision & Creative Tech or Engineering & Core AI.

Where can I see when a performer is on stage?

Open the performer's profile. It shows the day, time and stage of their session, and you can save it from there.

Will the talks be recorded?

Keynotes in the Prompt & Vision Hall are recorded and shared with ticket holders within 48 hours. Workshops are in person only.
