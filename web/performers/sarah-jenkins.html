Biography

Sarah Jenkins is a data engineer at PineScale, where she runs vector search clusters holding hundreds of millions of embeddings. She publishes reproducible benchmarks and shares her failures as openly as her results.

Vector Databases at Scale: HNSW Indexing Benchmarks for 100M Records

How does HNSW indexing behave at 100 million records? Sarah Jenkins shares reproducible benchmarks on recall, latency and memory, including the configurations that looked good and then collapsed.
