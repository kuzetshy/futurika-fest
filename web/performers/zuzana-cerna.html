Biography

Zuzana Černá is UX director at the Prague Design Collective and a long-time accessibility advocate. She co-designs interfaces with blind and low-vision creators and audits AI tools for inclusive interaction.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 3 (23 May) · 12:30–13:15

Talk

Accessible AI UI: Designing Inclusive Prompts for Impaired Creators

Prompt boxes are not automatically accessible. Zuzana Černá shares findings from co-design sessions with blind and low-vision creators and gives practical guidance for building more inclusive AI interfaces.
