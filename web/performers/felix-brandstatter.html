Biography

Felix Brandstätter is chief technology officer at HoloStage Vienna, which builds volumetric displays for theatre and live events. He started out as a lighting technician at the Vienna opera houses.

Real-Time AI Holography: Projecting Volumetric Synthetic Humans

Felix Brandstätter projects a volumetric synthetic performer live in the hall. He explains the display technology and rendering pipeline, and addresses when a digital human stops being comfortable to watch.
