Biography

Marcus Vance founded HoloMesh Labs after years as a technical artist in games. The company builds tools that turn rough text briefs into editable 3D assets for small production studios.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 1 (21 May) · 10:30–11:15

Talk

From Diffusion to Reality: The Evolution of Text-to-3D Synthesis

Text-to-3D has moved from blurry experiments to assets that can ship in a real project. Marcus Vance traces that progress and shows which steps of a production pipeline still need an artist.
