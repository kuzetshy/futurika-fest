Biography

Alexei Voronov is a complexity scientist at DataMind who models how norms spread through populations. Lately he has applied the same methods to help novelists and game writers test their fictional societies.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 3 (23 May) · 11:30–12:15

Talk

Multi-Agent Simulation of Fictional Societies for Worldbuilding

What would your fictional society do during a famine or an election? Alexei Voronov shows how multi-agent simulations help writers and game designers test the logic of the worlds they build.
