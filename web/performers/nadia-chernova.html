Biography

Nadia Chernova is a philosopher of technology at the Paris Centre for Philosophy of Technology. Her writing on authorship and intention has been widely discussed among artists who work with machine learning.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 2 (22 May) · 18:30–19:15

Talk

The Synthetic Muse: Can Neural Networks Possess Intention?

Can a system without desires be said to intend anything? Nadia Chernova ends the day's main-stage programme with a clear philosophical argument about authorship, aimed at artists and sceptics alike.
