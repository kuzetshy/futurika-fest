Biography

Matěj Dvořák is an intellectual property attorney advising studios and model developers across Central Europe. He helps creators draft licences for training data and fine-tuned model checkpoints.

The Economics of Generative IP: Licensing Neural Checkpoints

A fine-tuned checkpoint can carry value and legal risk at the same time. Matěj Dvořák explains how licences for models and datasets work in practice and what creators should check before signing.
