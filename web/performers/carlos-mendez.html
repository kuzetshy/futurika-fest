Biography

Carlos Mendez performs as a visual jockey at festivals across Europe and Latin America. He writes his own audio-reactive software and releases most of it under open-source licences.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 3 (23 May) · 09:30–10:15

Performance

Live Latent VJing: Real-time Audio-Reactive Video Synthesis

Start the final day with a live audiovisual set. Carlos Mendez generates video in real time from the music, then opens up his software to show how the performance works.
