Biography

Arthur Pendelton is a privacy architect at OpenSecure and previously worked with hospitals on shared research data. He designs federated training systems that satisfy both engineers and data protection officers.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 2 (22 May) · 12:30–13:15

Talk

Federated Learning: Collaborative Model Training Without Data Sharing

Hospitals and banks often cannot share raw data, yet they still want better models. Arthur Pendelton explains how federated learning works and where privacy guarantees remain weaker than they sound.
