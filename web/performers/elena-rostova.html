Biography

Elena Rostova spent a decade restoring archive film before moving into machine vision, and now leads the creative AI team at Synthetix. Her studio work tests how far style transfer can go before audiences stop trusting what they see on screen.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 1 (21 May) · 09:30–10:15

Keynote

Cognitive Aesthetics & Neural Style Transfer in Modern Cinema

Style transfer can now restyle a whole feature film, but viewers notice more than engineers expect. Elena Rostova opens the festival with audience research and restored archive footage, showing what makes a machine-altered image feel right or wrong.
