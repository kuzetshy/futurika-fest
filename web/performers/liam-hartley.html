Biography

Liam Hartley studies how synthetic media affects evidence, journalism and consent. As a research fellow at the Institute for Synthetic Media Ethics, he advises newsrooms on provenance and labelling standards.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 1 (21 May) · 12:30–13:15

Talk

Ethics of Synthetic Media: Deepfakes, Watermarking, and Ownership

Who owns a synthetic likeness, and can a watermark survive contact with the internet? Liam Hartley compares current provenance methods and explains where they protect people and where they fail.
