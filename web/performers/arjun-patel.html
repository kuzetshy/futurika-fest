Biography

Arjun Patel leads extended reality research at NeuraVision, working on headsets that generate scenes as the wearer moves. He previously built real-time rendering engines for flight simulators.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 1 (21 May) · 18:30–19:15

Demo

Interactive Spatial Computing with Real-time Diffusion Engines

Arjun Patel closes the first day on the main stage with a headset demo that generates its surroundings as the wearer moves. He breaks down the latency budget that keeps the experience comfortable.
