Biography

Eliška Procházková is a DevOps architect at CloudPeak, running distributed platforms for European retailers. Her current work gives observability agents limited permission to diagnose and repair incidents.

Session at Futurika Fest

Talk

Self-Healing Distributed Systems Powered by Observability Agents

Eliška Procházková shows what happens when observability agents get limited permission to act. The talk covers the guardrails, approval steps and rollback rules behind automatic incident repair in a production platform.
