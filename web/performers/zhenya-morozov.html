Biography

Zhenya Morozov curates FutureLens Gallery, which shows photography and image synthesis side by side. Their exhibitions ask visitors to guess how each picture was made and record what changes their mind.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 2 (22 May) · 11:30–12:15

Talk

Post-Photography: Authenticity in the Age of Midjourney v8

If an image can be generated in seconds, what still makes a photograph a record? Zhenya Morozov draws on gallery experiments in which visitors judged authenticity, often to their own surprise.
