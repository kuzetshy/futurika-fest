Biography

Evelyn Reed is an independent film director whose last two features combined human actors with AI-driven background characters. She teaches directing workshops on running a set where some of the cast are software.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 3 (23 May) · 14:30–15:15

Keynote

Human-in-the-Loop Filmmaking: Directing Agentic Casts

How do you direct an actor who is an agent? In the final day's keynote, Evelyn Reed shares scenes and failures from two independent features and argues for keeping human judgement at the centre of filmmaking.
