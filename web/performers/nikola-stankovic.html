Biography

Nikola Stanković is a hardware engineer at NeuroChip, working on spiking neural processors for battery-powered sensors. He designed his first low-power boards for agricultural monitoring.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 3 (23 May) · 12:30–13:15

Talk

Neuromorphic Computing: Spiking Neural Networks in Low-Power IoT

Spiking neural networks use energy only when something happens. Nikola Stanković explains neuromorphic chips and shows how they extend battery life in low-power IoT sensors in the field.
