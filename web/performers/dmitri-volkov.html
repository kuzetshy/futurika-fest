Biography

Dmitri Volkov is lead architect at Agentic Stack and has spent years making probabilistic systems dependable enough for regulated industries. He is a strong advocate of typed interfaces between models and code.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 1 (21 May) · 11:30–12:15

Talk

Building Deterministic Workflows on Top of Non-Deterministic LLMs

Dependable software needs predictable behaviour, yet language models are probabilistic. Dmitri Volkov shows how typed interfaces, validation layers and retries turn uncertain outputs into workflows that regulated teams can trust.
