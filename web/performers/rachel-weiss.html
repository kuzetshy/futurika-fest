Biography

Rachel Weiss is a choreographer and roboticist at the Zurich Arts Lab. Her duets pair trained dancers with robotic arms that learn phrasing through rehearsal rather than fixed programming.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 3 (23 May) · 18:30–19:15

Performance

Embodied AI in Performance Art: Human-Robot Dance Duets

The festival's closing performance on the main stage is a duet for a dancer and a robotic arm. Rachel Weiss then explains how the machine learned its phrasing through rehearsal.
