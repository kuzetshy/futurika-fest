Biography

Oskar Blomqvist calls himself a digital horticulturist. At Nordic Green he grows simulated gardens for museums and botanic centres, with species modelled on real growth and pollination data.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 3 (23 May) · 13:30–14:15

Demo

Synthetic Botany: Algorithmic Flora Simulation for Interactive Exhibits

Oskar Blomqvist grows a simulated garden on stage and invites the audience to change the climate. He explains how real botanical data makes digital plants behave believably.
