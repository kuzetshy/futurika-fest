Biography

Martin Svoboda heads AI at SearchBridge, a Brno company building retrieval systems for technical documentation. He joined from enterprise search and remains sceptical of any demo without an evaluation set.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 1 (21 May) · 15:30–16:15

Talk

RAG That Doesn't Hallucinate: Graph Neural Networks meet Dense Retrieval

Dense retrieval alone often returns plausible but wrong context. Martin Svoboda explains how combining it with graph neural networks reduced hallucinations in a technical documentation assistant, with the evaluation data to prove it.
