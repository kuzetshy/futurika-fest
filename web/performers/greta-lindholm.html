Biography

Greta Lindholm leads game design at Nordic Worlds, an independent studio known for open-world survival titles. She focuses on systems where quests and characters emerge from play instead of being scripted.

Session at Futurika Fest

Day 2 (22 May) · 09:30–10:15

Keynote

AI in Game Design: Generating Living Worlds without Static Assets

What happens when a game world is generated while you play it? Greta Lindholm opens the second day with lessons from a studio replacing static assets with systems, and explains why designers matter more than ever.
