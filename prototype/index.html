Practical Information & FAQ

Everything you need to know about venue access, transportation, accessibility, and summit guidelines.

📍 Location & Directions

DOX Centre for Contemporary Art & DOX+ Hall
Poupětova 1, Prague 7 – Holešovice, Czech Republic

• Metro: Line C (Red) to Nádraží Holešovice (6 min walk)
• Tram: Lines 6, 12, 14 to stop Dělnická or Ortenovo náměstí (2 min walk)
• Train: Prague-Holešovice Train Station (8 min walk)

♿ Accessibility & Inclusivity

We are dedicated to providing a fully accessible experience conforming to WCAG 2.0 AA digital guidelines and physical standards:

  • Step-free access to all entrances and DOX+ hall.
  • Elevator access to Innovation Hub Floor 2.
  • Accessible restrooms on Ground Floor & Floor 2.
  • Induction hearing loops installed in Vision Hall.
